## 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm **Joshua Porunnedath Biju**, a **Master's student in Applied Computer Science** at **Hochschule Schmalkalden**, specializing in **IT Security**, currently working as a **Working Student in Information Security & IT Compliance at Hugo Boss AG**.

I combine hands-on experience in **GRC, IAM, and ISMS operations** with a genuine interest in **Red Teaming, IoT Security, and AI Security**. My Master's thesis, developed in collaboration with Hugo Boss AG and supervised by the CISO, is building a multi-agent AI system for enterprise phishing detection with Human-in-the-Loop security controls.
